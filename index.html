Mathematical Foundation: Non-Linear Capillary State Inversion

Loop Heat Pipes (LHPs) rely on passive capillary meniscus forces inside a sub-micron porous wick to circulate vapor to remote deployable radiators without mechanical pumps. The LoopHeat Auditor solves the hydrodynamic-thermodynamic boundary equations in real time, projecting state coordinates onto the $P\text{-}E$ phase envelope to detect dry-out bifurcation prior to thermal runaway.

Card 01 Laplace-Young Equilibrium

1. Governing Capillary Pumping Limit

Circulation persists if and only if the maximum capillary head generated across the fine-pore primary wick exceeds the sum of all loop frictional, viscous, and hydrostatic pressure drops across lines and condensers:

$$\Delta P_{c,\max} = \frac{2\sigma \cos\theta}{r_p} \ge \sum \Delta P_i = \Delta P_v + \Delta P_l + \Delta P_{\text{cond}} + \Delta P_w$$

Where $\sigma$ is fluid surface tension ($N/m$), $\theta$ is contact wetting angle, $r_p$ is effective pore radius ($1.2\,\mu\text{m}$ for sintered titanium), and $\Delta P_v, \Delta P_l, \Delta P_{\text{cond}}, \Delta P_w$ are friction losses through vapor transport, liquid return, condenser tubing, and wick matrix.

Failure Criterion: When $\sum \Delta P_i > \Delta P_{c,\max}$, menisci recede into the wick body, vapor breaches the core, and hydraulic pumping ceases.
Card 02 Clapeyron Enthalpy Balance

2. Phase-Change Mass Flux & Heat Balance

Applied science payload heat $E$ ($W$) splits between latent vapor vaporization at the outer wick circumference and parasitic heat leak conduction ($Q_{\text{leak}}$) backwards through the wick into the compensation chamber:

$$E = \dot{m} h_{fg} + Q_{\text{leak}} = \dot{m} h_{fg} + \frac{2\pi k_{\text{eff}} L_{\text{wick}}}{\ln(r_o/r_i)} (T_{\text{evap}} - T_{\text{cc}})$$

With mass flow rate $\dot{m} = \frac{E - Q_{\text{leak}}}{h_{fg}}$. As payload power surges or subcooling degrades, $Q_{\text{leak}}$ drives compensation chamber temperature $T_{\text{cc}}$ to match $T_{\text{evap}}$, collapsing saturation pressure differential.

Thermodynamic Insight: Parasitic leak increases superheat in the compensation chamber, precipitating two-phase vapor lock.
Card 03 Real-Time Auditor Inversion

3. The $P\text{-}E$ Phase-Space Inversion Law

Loop pressure differential exhibits quadratic Hagen-Poiseuille and Ergun porous media scaling with respect to thermal power ($E$). The software continuously computes the instantaneous differential derivative $\frac{dE}{dP}$:

$$\Delta P(E) = \frac{\mu_v E}{\rho_v h_{fg} K_v} + \frac{\beta_v E^2}{\rho_v h_{fg}^2} + \frac{\mu_l E}{\rho_l h_{fg} K_l}$$

Under nominal conditions, $\frac{dE}{dP} > \kappa_{\text{threshold}}$. When approaching the capillary dry-out inflection, $\frac{dE}{dP} \to 0$, signaling that additional pressure head generates zero added heat transport—the geometric signature of impending de-prime.

Autonomous Trigger: If $\nabla_{P,E} \le \epsilon_{\text{safe}}$, initiate payload cycle-skipping and secondary reservoir trim heaters.
